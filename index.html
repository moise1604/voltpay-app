FAQs
How do I add a meter?
Go to My Meters, tap the + button, select the country, enter the serial number on the meter label, verify it, give it a nickname and save.
Which countries are supported?
Rwanda, Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania and DR Congo. More countries coming soon.
How long does a recharge take?
Mobile Money recharges are instant. Card payments via PesaPal can take 1-3 minutes.
Is my payment secure?
Yes. All transactions are encrypted. Card payments go through PesaPal (PCI-DSS certified). We never store your card details.
